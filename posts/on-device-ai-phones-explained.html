"On-device AI" is the biggest phone buzzword of 2026. New phones advertise AI assistants, live translation and smart photo editing that run on the phone itself. But what does that actually change for you?

On-device vs cloud AI

On-deviceCloud
Works offlineYesNo
PrivacyData stays on phoneData sent to servers
SpeedInstant for small tasksDepends on network
PowerLimited by phone chipMuch more powerful

Most phones use both: small, quick tasks run on the phone, and bigger requests go to the cloud.

Features that usually run on the phone

  • Call screening and spam detection
  • Live captions and voice typing
  • Photo cleanup (removing objects, fixing blur)
  • Summarising notifications and messages
  • Offline translation for downloaded languages

What to check before buying

  • RAM: on-device AI needs memory. 12 GB or more gives room to grow.
  • Chip: look for a recent flagship or upper mid-range processor with a dedicated AI engine (NPU).
  • Update promise: AI features arrive through updates, so longer software support matters more than ever.
  • Which features are free: some brands put advanced AI features behind a subscription.
Phones with strong on-device AI

Look for 12 GB+ RAM, a recent processor with an NPU, and at least 5 years of updates.

Should it change what you buy?

If privacy and offline use matter to you, yes. Otherwise, battery life, camera and update support still matter more day to day. Treat on-device AI as a bonus, not the main reason to upgrade.
